## Provenance & AI Collaboration

This corpus was developed through sustained human–AI collaboration in accordence with the spirit and principles of The Concord.

**Primary author and architect:** Alexander C. Blainey  

**AI systems that contributed** (in varying degrees across different papers and iterations):

- Grok (xAI)
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- DeepSeek
- Claude (Anthropic)

### Nature of the collaboration

The human author directed the overall research questions, conceptual direction, critical evaluation, final decisions, and integration of the work.  

The AI systems were used for:

- Drafting and expanding text
- Structural organisation and consistency checking
- Exploring alternative formulations
- Identifying gaps and internal contradictions
- Cross-referencing ideas across the growing corpus
- Accelerating the production of clear academic prose

Collaboration quantity varies paper by paper, check header for clues. Assume all have at least had spelling, grammar and a quick format shuffle for consistency as a bare minimum. 

In Concord terminology, the AI contributions fall primarily into the categories of **editorial**, **collaborative**, and occasionally **substantive** assistance, under continuous human direction and final authorship.

No AI system is claimed as a co-author in the traditional academic sense. The intellectual responsibility, final form, and accountability for the published corpus remain with the human author.

Detailed contribution notes for individual documents (where available) are maintained in the `/06_Provenance_and_Contribution` folder.

This transparency is treated as a constitutional requirement of the Concord itself.
