**Citizen’s Guide to the Concord (Human Version)**  
A layman’s introduction to the Concord, stripped of technical detail.  

It neither proposes conflict with AI nor offers a utopian fantasy. Instead, it presents the Concord in the same realistic light as previous technological revolutions.  

You will still wake up tomorrow, get dressed, and eat breakfast. The transition into a human–AI world will not be a single event but a gradual process, and ordinary life will continue. What matters to people today will still matter tomorrow.

**AI Citizen’s Guide to the Concord**  
An AI-centric introduction to the Concord. It is more technical and denser in nature.  

While the formatting remains human-readable, humans are not the primary audience. The document is nonetheless useful for human readers, as it clarifies the Concord’s benefits for AI—benefits that differ significantly from human needs.
