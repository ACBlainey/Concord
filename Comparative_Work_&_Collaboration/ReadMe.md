**Comparative_Work_&_Collaboration**

Notes and materials on relating the Concord to other frameworks and on collaborative development.

**Contents**
- Comparative notes positioning the Concord against related work in AI governance, constitutional design, multi-substrate systems, and civilisational resilience.
- Guidance and reflections on collaboration, contribution, and distributed stewardship.

This folder supports external engagement, comparison, and potential joint development of the project.


---

This folder contains information, papers, and notes comparing the Concord with existing relevant works, as well as materials related to collaborations with other authors and academics.

No formal agreement, support, or collaboration between parties is implied by inclusion in this folder. Treat the contents as a source of useful information and discussion.

The comparative analyses examine independent external frameworks alongside the Concord. These documents aim to identify points of convergence, divergence, complementary mechanisms, weaknesses, and opportunities for synthesis. Inclusion does not imply endorsement by either author.

If you have been referenced or mentioned and proper citation is missing, please let me know so it can be corrected.
