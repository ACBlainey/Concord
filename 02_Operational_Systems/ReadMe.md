**02_Operational_Systems**

Operational subsystems that implement the Concord’s core principles.

**Contents**
- **Governance** — Triadic decision-making, distributed stewardship, layered authority.

- **Judiciary** — Fiduciary justice, constitutional review, auditing.

- **Continuity Protocol** — Long-term institutional and knowledge continuity.

- **Emergency Systems** — Constitutional crisis response and recovery.

- **Knowledge Control System (KCS)** — Provenance, integrity, and controlled sharing of knowledge.

- **Ratchet** — Economic and monetary architecture; community economy and proof of useful contribution.

These documents translate the ethical and constitutional foundations into practical governance, justice, continuity, knowledge, emergency, and economic mechanisms.
