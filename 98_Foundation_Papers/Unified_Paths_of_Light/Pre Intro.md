---

## Pre-Introduction to the Project

This work is the second edition of *"Unified Paths of Light"*, which established the core ethical principles known as Blainey's Laws, of the Concord Edition One.

The unified paths have now been renamed **Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity** in order to move away slightly from the metaphysical domain the project originally started in. This is to avoid readers mistaking it for a purely theological paper.

---

## Scope Expansion

In this second edition, the scope has been expanded to include the world's eight largest and most enduring religions. The selection differs slightly from the first edition, most notably by incorporating:

| Addition | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **Chinese Confucianism** | Within the broader category of Chinese folk religions; introduces East Asian moral perspectives |
| **African Traditional Religions** | Introduces indigenous African moral traditions |

These two additions are intended to introduce greater cultural and philosophical diversity into the extraction process, enriching the resulting ethical core with perspectives from East Asian and indigenous African moral traditions.

---

## Anticipated Outcome

Despite these changes in scope and source material, I anticipate that the final extracted ethical principles will closely resemble the original **Blainey's Laws**—the condensed ethical framework derived from the first *Unified Paths of Light* project.

### Significance of Anticipated Convergence

> **This convergence, if confirmed, would suggest that the core ethical principles necessary for social stability and civilisational longevity are not culturally arbitrary but are, in fact, universally discoverable across humanity's most enduring moral traditions.**
