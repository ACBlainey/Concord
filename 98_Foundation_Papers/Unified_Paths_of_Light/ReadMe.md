**Note on the Second Edition**

The Second Edition of *Unified Paths of Light* (later renamed *Sacred Codes and Civilizational Longevity*) produced a slightly different core set of Blainey’s Laws — or, arguably, a refined interpretation of the underlying ethics.

It remains unclear whether the difference arises from changes in the religious texts and traditions examined, or from a more refined methodology.  

An important open question is how this variant of Blainey’s Laws performs when applied to the Concord, and whether it produces meaningful differences in principles, structure, or outcomes.

---

**Suggested extensions**

- Reverse the comparison methodology: begin with another tradition (e.g., Qur’anic verses) and search for correlations, rather than starting from the Psalms.  
- Ideally, apply the same method to all selected traditions and examine the aggregate results for a more universal set of Blainey’s Laws.  
- Additional traditions may be included. The original *Unified Paths of Light* examined Jainism; it was omitted from the Second Edition due to relatively low adherent numbers, despite its notable longevity.
